Turn it on to author your own notebooks from scratch and keep your work across sessions. Enabling will:

  • Fork the public StargazerBio/stargazer repo into your GitHub account.
  • Send you to GitHub to install the Stargazer app — choose Only select repositories and pick your new stargazer fork, so it can read and write just that one repo.
  • Save notebooks you create or edit to your fork's main, under notebooks/workspace/.

Stargazer never holds a broad token — the app is scoped to your fork, and you can revoke it anytime from your GitHub settings. Saving starts working once the app is installed; nothing is written until then. Tutorials and Workflow notebooks work without it — they just aren't saved.
